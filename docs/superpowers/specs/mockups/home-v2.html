03Named for Amelia

A grandmother's kitchen, an all-day table.

Amelia's is named for founder Stacey Weber's grandmother — the cook who taught her that real food, made from scratch and shared with heart, is how you take care of people. It's a community gathering place for consciously curated food in a casual room. Families always welcome.
